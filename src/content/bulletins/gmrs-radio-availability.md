The two GMRS radios we ask new members to buy, the Baofeng GM-15 Pro and the Baofeng UV-5G, are no longer listed on Amazon. In mid-November, radio hobbyists began reporting that many Baofeng listings had disappeared, and the product pages we linked from our [GMRS Guide](https://ersn.net/gmrs) are now gone.

## What We Know

- **The removals are real, but uneven.** Some Baofeng models and sellers are still listed, while others have vanished entirely.
- **Nobody has explained why.** We have not found a statement from Amazon, Baofeng, or the FCC. There is plenty of speculation online, including talk of an FCC crackdown, but we have not found any FCC action that names these radios, so we are not going to guess at the cause.
- **We don't know if they are coming back.** The listings could return next week or not at all.

## If You Already Have a Radio

**Nothing changes for you.** Your GM-15 Pro or UV-5G still works on our repeaters and nets, and there is no reason to replace it. Keep it charged and keep checking in.

## If You Need a Radio Now

We don't have official replacement recommendations yet. **If you can wait until December, please wait.**

If you need a radio sooner, these are GMRS-specific, repeater-capable handhelds with programmable channels, sold by established US dealers. We have not tested or programmed them ourselves yet, so setup may take more effort than with the radios we have been using.

- **[Wouxun KG-805G](https://www.buytwowayradios.com/wouxun-kg-805g.html):** About $80 from Buy Two Way Radios. Simple, sturdy, and in stock when we checked.
- **[Wouxun KG-935G Plus](https://www.buytwowayradios.com/wouxun-kg-935g-plus.html):** About $120 from Buy Two Way Radios. More features and better weather resistance. Backordered into early December when we checked.
- **[BTECH GMRS-V2](https://baofengtech.com/product/gmrs-v2/):** About $65 direct from BTECH. The closest relative of the radios we have been using. Backordered into mid-December when we checked.

A few things to watch for while shopping:

- **Make sure it is a GMRS radio.** Many similar-looking radios, including the popular Baofeng UV-5R, are ham radios that are not certified for GMRS.
- **Check with us before buying off the shelf.** GMRS radios sold in stores by brands like Midland often support only a limited set of tones and custom channels, and may not work with our repeaters.
- **Don't overpay.** Be wary of marked-up resale listings for the radios that have disappeared.

Not sure about a radio? Email [ersnnets@gmail.com](mailto:ersnnets@gmail.com) before you buy and we will help you check.

## What Happens Next

We are testing alternatives and will post updated recommendations in **December**, both in our news and in the [GMRS Guide](https://ersn.net/gmrs), which carries the current interim list in the meantime.
